Who We Are

Connecting Young Leaders Across Borders

Global Youth Network (GYN) is an international organization dedicated to connecting and empowering young people worldwide. We bring together students, professionals, and innovators to collaborate across borders, develop leadership skills, and create meaningful global impact.

Following the remarkable success of our inaugural summit in Bangkok, Thailand, the journey continues in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia — a vibrant hub of innovation, education, and sustainable development.

SDG Innovation Summit Malaysia 2026

Organized by GYN and co-hosted by the United Nations University – International Institute for Global Health, Malaysia (UNU Global Health). Join 100+ young leaders, changemakers, students, and professionals in Kuala Lumpur for a global youth leadership summit.

  • Dates 19–22 November 2026
  • Venue Teedo Hotel, Kuala Lumpur
  • Seats 100 total
  • Theme Innovative Ideas for Social Impact
  • Co-host UNU Global Health, Malaysia
