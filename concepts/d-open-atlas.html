From Bangkok to Kuala Lumpur

Each GYN summit is a chapter in a longer story. Edition 01 planted the flag in Southeast Asia. Edition 02 raises it higher.

01
Past Edition

Bangkok

13.7563° N, 100.5018° E   |  July 16–19, 2026   |   Le Srivaree Suvarnabhumi Hotel

The inaugural GYN summit. Over 70 participants from across the world gathered in Bangkok under the theme Exploring Sustainability through Innovative Minds. Four chapters of dialogue, workshops and policy roundtables.

Chapter 1

Global Collaboration & Leadership

Global leaders discuss how collaboration and leadership drive innovation across sustainable development.

  • Keynote From Local Action to Global Impact — Kehkashan Basu
  • Interactive Keynote MDGs to SDGs to 2045 — Dr. Suman Roy
  • Keynote Advancing Women's Rights — Dr. Methinee Wongwanich
Chapter 2

Connecting Strategy, Policy & Social Impact

Workshops and keynotes on policy formulation and strategic decision-making for sustainable futures.

  • Policy Workshop Transforming Education through Inclusive Policies — Dr. Mona Al Kadi
  • Entrepreneurship Workshop Profit, People, Planet — Sura Fitiani
  • Keynote Feminist Climate Governance — Sidra Riaz
Chapter 3 — Summit Theme

Exploring Sustainability through Innovative Minds

The delegate-led heart of the summit. Teams formed, brainstormed and presented solutions to a panel of judges.

  • Delegate Activity World Got a Problem — Group Formation, Brainstorming & Presentations
Chapter 4

AI Corner: Smart Innovation, Human Rights & AI

A dedicated space exploring artificial intelligence, innovation and human rights with tech leaders and sustainability experts.

  • Keynote AI as Sustainable Digital Infrastructure — Wenbo Li
  • Workshop Smart Innovation: AI & Gender Equality — Dr. Shaleen Das
  • AI Panel Future Minds & a Sustainable Future — moderated by Sura Fitiani

Who We Are

Global Youth Network

Global Youth Network (GYN) is an international organization dedicated to connecting and empowering young people worldwide. We bring together students, professionals, and innovators to collaborate across borders, develop leadership skills, and create meaningful global impact.

Conferences
GYN hosts international forums, workshops and summits to promote dialogue on Sustainable Development Goals.
Networking
A collaborative ecosystem of mentorship, technical support and resource sharing to address the world's most pressing challenges.
Leadership Development
Empowering a new generation of leaders to navigate complexity with visionary foresight and measurable global impact.
Collaborative Networks
Cultivating a global ecosystem of cross-border dialogue and strategic partnerships for innovative solutions.
Youth Mentoring
Providing tailor-made and hands-on mentoring to youth to become effective leaders.
Workshops & Trainings
Building leadership capacities and policy expertise for future-ready governance.
Our Objectives
01
Empowerment
Priming young leaders with skills, mindset, and confidence to drive systemic change.
02
Data Unification
Collect and unify data and research related to SDGs 4, 5, 9 and 13 from across the globe.
03
Research Exploration
Exploring fields, sharing research, and volunteer work across borders.
04
Capacity Building
Bringing scientists, students, NGOs and legal professionals together through sessions and trainings.
05
Policy Round-ups
Bridging gaps between AI experts, businesses, NGOs and volunteers through policy and ideas.
06
Sustainable Impact
Advancing SDGs 4, 5, 9 and 13 through youth-led innovation and leadership skills.
