Who we are and what we do

Global Youth Network (GYN) is an international organization dedicated to connecting young people worldwide. We bring together students, professionals, and innovators to collaborate across borders, develop leadership skills, and create meaningful global impact.

  • Conferences

    GYN hosts international forums, workshops and summits to promote dialogue on Sustainable Development Goals

  • Networking

    Providing a collaborative platform of mentorship, technical support and resource sharing to address the world's most pressing challenges

  • Leadership Development

    Priming young leaders with skills, mindset, and confidence to drive systemic change

  • Collaborative Networks

    Cross-border dialogue and strategic partnerships for the world's most complex challenges

  • Youth Mentoring

    Providing tailor-made and hands-on mentoring to youth to become effective leaders

  • Workshops & Trainings

    Building leadership capacities and policy expertise for future-ready governance

Common questions

When are scholarship results announced?

Results will be announced on 16 October 2026. All applicants will receive an email, and the final list will be posted on GYN Facebook and Instagram.

Can I attend if not selected for a scholarship?

Yes. You can join via the Self-Funded Package ($399) or the Forum Access Package ($199). You will enjoy the same full experience and privileges as all other participants.

Does GYN provide visa assistance?

GYN will provide an official invitation letter, proof of accommodation, and the programme location. The final visa decision rests with the embassy.

How do I pay the application fee?

You can pay securely by debit or credit card, or Google Pay, through a trusted Stripe payment gateway.

$1,000 seed grant award

As part of the SDG Innovation Summit Malaysia 2026, participants will compete for a $1,000 Seed Grant to support innovative projects and social impact initiatives. Whether your project is at the conceptual stage, in its pilot phase, currently under implementation, or fully realized, we welcome you to present your innovation on stage.

Institutional visit: Asia Pacific University

Experience an exclusive institutional visit to Asia Pacific University (APU), one of Malaysia's leading universities. Delegates will also explore Petaling Street (Chinatown), KLCC Park, and many other spots in Kuala Lumpur.

The SDG Innovation Summit Malaysia 2026 is co-hosted by the United Nations University, International Institute for Global Health, Malaysia. Participants will engage with UNU Global Health representatives and UNU Young Leaders during the summit.
