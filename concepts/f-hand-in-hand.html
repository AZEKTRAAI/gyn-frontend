Who we are

Global Youth Network (GYN) is an international organization dedicated to connecting young people worldwide. We bring together students, professionals, and innovators to collaborate across borders, develop leadership skills, and create meaningful global impact.

Six things we do

Conferences

International forums, workshops and summits to promote dialogue on the SDGs

Networking

A collaborative network of mentorship, technical support and resource sharing

Leadership development

Building leaders with the skills and confidence to drive systemic change

Collaborative networks

Cross-border dialogue and strategic partnerships for complex global challenges

Youth mentoring

Tailor-made, hands-on mentoring to help young people become effective leaders

Workshops & trainings

Building leadership capacity and policy expertise for future-ready governance

SDG Innovation Summit Malaysia 2026

The SDG Innovation Summit Malaysia 2026 brings together 100 young leaders, innovators, entrepreneurs, students, professionals, and changemakers from around the world.

Co-hosted by UNU Global Health, Malaysia

$1,000 seed grant

The best team project wins a $1,000 seed grant to launch their idea.

Institutional visit: Asia Pacific University (APU), Malaysia

100 participants. 4 days. Teedo Hotel, Kuala Lumpur.

Exchange ideas, build connections, and drive sustainable impact aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Four-day programme

Day 1, Nov 19
Arrival, hotel check-in, meet and greet, explore Kuala Lumpur
Day 2, Nov 20
Opening, workshops on the SDGs and social ventures, panel discussion
Day 3, Nov 21
Policy impact workshop, SDG case studies, delegate presentations, cultural night
Day 4, Nov 22
Certification ceremony, group photo, check-out
