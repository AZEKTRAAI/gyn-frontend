WHO WE ARE

Global Youth Network (GYN) is an international organization dedicated to connecting and empowering young people worldwide. We bring together students, professionals, and innovators to collaborate across borders, develop leadership skills, and create meaningful global impact.

Conferences

GYN hosts international forums, workshops and summits to promote dialogue on Sustainable Development Goals

Networking

The GYN provides a collaborative platform of mentorship, technical support and resource sharing to address the world's most pressing challenges

Leadership development

Priming a new generation of leaders to navigate complexity with visionary foresight, unwavering integrity and measurable global impact

Collaborative networks

Cultivating cross-border dialogue and strategic partnerships to address the world's most complex challenges

Youth mentoring

Providing tailor-made and hands-on mentoring to youth to become effective leaders

Workshops & trainings

Building leadership capacities and policy expertise for future-ready governance

APU CAMPUS VISIT

Experience an exclusive institutional visit to Asia Pacific University (APU), one of Malaysia's leading universities. Explore its academic environment and global learning community while connecting with fellow international delegates.

Delegates will also explore Petaling Street, GMBB KL, Petronas Twin Towers, KLCC Park and many other spots.

The Global Youth Network (GYN) organizes the SDG Innovation Summit Malaysia, co-hosted by the United Nations University International Institute for Global Health, Malaysia. Participants will engage with UNU Global Health representatives and UNU Young Leaders during the summit.
