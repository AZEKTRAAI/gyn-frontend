Round 2: four new directions for GYN

The client pointed us at echochange.org, so this round goes louder: solid colour blocks, the 17 SDG colours as the palette, real delegate photos up front, and 3D animation rendered in Blender (actual renders, not CSS effects). We also checked round 1 against a published list of what makes a site look AI-made, and built these four to avoid every item on it.

    What changed from round 1

    • The palette moved from navy and cream to full SDG colour: solid crimson, cyan, sun yellow, magenta, ocean blue and green blocks, with the logo's four hand colours at the centre.
    • Each concept has a Blender render that means something for GYN: the SDG wheel, the four hands from the logo, a glass globe with 17 goal colours, and the GYN letters with stickers. On Safari and for anyone who turns motion off, they show as still images.
    • We removed the patterns that mark a site as AI-made: purple, gradients, frosted glass, the badge above the headline, numbered section labels, identical card grids, fade-in on every element, and stock phrases like "empower" and "elevate". The full list with sources is in docs/ai-tells.md.
    • None of the round 1 typefaces are used again, because those were on the list too.
