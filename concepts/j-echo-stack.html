The programme

Four days, hour by hour.

Day 1, 19 Nov

Arrive, check in, meet everyone

Hotel check-in, delegate registration and your welcome kit. Then the evening is yours to explore KL.

Day 2, 20 Nov

Country showcase

Ice-breaking, delegate introductions and a showcase of where everyone comes from.

Day 2, 20 Nov

Workshops 1 and 2

Understanding the SDGs, then Social Ventures. A panel, and the first Summit Entrepreneurship Activity.

Day 2, 20 Nov

Free city tour

Petaling Street, GMBB KL, the Petronas Twin Towers and KLCC Park with the other delegates.

Day 3, 21 Nov

Workshops 3 and 4

Policy Impact, then SDG Case Studies. Entrepreneurship Activity B follows.

Day 3, 21 Nov

Hear You Out: your presentation

Youth-led presentations. This is the stage, and the $1,000 seed grant pitch.

Day 3, 21 Nov

Cultural night

Performances, food and music from every country in the room, then dinner.

Day 4, 22 Nov

Certification ceremony

Your participation certificate, the group photo, and check-out. The APU campus visit sits in the programme too.
