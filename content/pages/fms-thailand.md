### FutureMinds Summit Thailand (FMST)

* Bangkok

* July 16–19, 2026

The FutureMinds Summit Thailand was the **first and inaugural event** organized by the Global Youth Network.

Held in **Bangkok** from **July 16–19, 2026**, the event brought together over **70 participants** from around the world.

Participants took part in engaging sessions, interactive workshops, and team-building activities throughout the program. They also **enjoyed a sightseeing tour** to Asiatique, where they immersed themselves in the city’s vibrant atmosphere, explored local shops, and experienced a scenic river cruise.

Renowned speakers — most notably **H.E. Mr. Salman Pervaiz, Dr. Suman Roy, and Ms. Ashley Blake** — were also part of the FutureMinds Summit.

Day 1 began with the opening ceremony, and Day 3 concluded with a cultural performance and certificate distribution.

## Four Days on the Ground

The signature activities that ran alongside the chapters, from the first icebreaker to the closing photo.

16 July

Day 1 Inaugural Evening

Passport Bingo

An icebreaking challenge that sent delegates around the venue to find peers matching five prompts — from multilingual speakers to fellow travelers and gender-equality advocates — building the summit's first connections and a social-media trail under #FMST26.

17 July 2026

Day 2

World Got a Problem — Phase A

Delegates chose the SDG they were most passionate about, formed teams, elected a group representative, and identified one significant global problem to solve — with guest speakers and fellows on hand as mentors. An evening sightseeing tour (6:30–9:00 PM) gave the group a chance to unwind and take in Bangkok beyond the summit halls.

18 July

Day 3

World Got a Problem — Phase B: Present Your Solution

Each team took the stage for five to six minutes to present their proposed solution to a panel of judges, scored on innovation, relevance to their SDG, feasibility, sustainability, presentation and teamwork.

18 July

Day 3 Night

Certificates, "04:04" & Cultural Night

The summit closed with Certificates of Participation and a "04:04 — hear you out" reflection session, followed by the optional Cultural Night: a celebration of unity in diversity with traditional attire, an open dance floor, and a Rabindra Sangeet performance in Japanese, German and English by cultural director Dr. Dipa Das. The event ended with an official group photograph of the whole FMST 26' community.
