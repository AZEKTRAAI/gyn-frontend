##### **WHO WE ARE & WHAT WE DO**

Global Youth Network (GYN) is an international organization dedicated to connecting and empowering young people worldwide. We bring together students, professionals, and innovators to collaborate across borders, develop leadership skills, and create meaningful global impact.

Conferences

GYN hosts international forums, workshops and summits to promote dialogue on Sustainable Development Goals

Networking

The GYN empowers the next generation by providing a collaborative ecosystem of mentorship, technical support and resource sharing to address the world’s most pressing challenges

Leadership Development

Empowering a new generation of leaders to navigate complexity with visionary foresight, unwavering integrity and measurable
global impact

Collaborative Networks

Cultivating a global ecosystem of cross-border dialogue and strategic partnerships to unlock innovative solutions for the world’s most complex challenges

Youth Mentoring

Providing tailor-made and hands-on mentoring to youth to become effective leaders

Workshops & Trainings

Building leadership capacities and policy expertise for future-ready governance

OUR FOCUS AREAS

“At GYN, we focus on four Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) as education, gender equality, industry – innovation & Infrastructure, and climate action, uniting young leaders worldwide to create impactful solutions and drive positive change for a sustainable future.”

## 4 SDGs | 1 SUSTAINABLE FUTURE

Taking urgent action for our Climate.

## OBJECTIVES

### Empowerment

Priming young leaders with skills, mindset, and confidence to drive systemic change.

### Data Unification

To collect and unify data and research ideas from across the globe related to SDG 4, SDG 5, SDG 9 and SDG 13

### Research Exploration

Exploring fields, sharing research, and volunteer work

### Capacity Building

Bringing scientists, students, NGOs, and legal professionals together through sessions and trainings to foster innovation and peace.

### Policy Round-ups

To provide a platform for bridging gaps, bringing cross-functional ideas from AI experts, businesses, NGOs, and volunteers through policy & ideas

### Global Collaboration

Providing a forum for international networking (cross-border dialogue) and cultural exchange.

### Sustainable Impact

Advancing the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGS 4, 5, 9, and 13) through youth-led innovation and leadership skills.
