# Refund Policy

#### **REFUND POLICY**

At GYN, we are committed to delivering an exceptional experience for all conference delegates. We recognize that unforeseen circumstances may arise, making a refund request necessary. 

This Refund Policy outlines the terms, conditions, and procedures applicable to conference registrations purchased through our online platform.

#### **Refund Eligibility:**

All approved refunds will be issued exclusively as **GYN Credit Vouchers (GCVs)**. 

These vouchers may be redeemed only for registration fees related to future GYN conferences and events and are non-transferable unless otherwise stated.

Refund eligibility and applicable conditions vary according to the registration category, as detailed below:

#### **Scholarship Category Application Fees**

* **Registration Fees:** All scholarship registration fees are mandatory and non-refundable.
* By completing the payment, applicants acknowledge that these fees cannot be disputed under any circumstances.

* To ensure full transparency, all applicants for fully or partially funded programs must review and sign the **[Declaration of Terms and](https://thegyn.org/terms-conditions/)**[**Conditions**](https://thegyn.org/terms-conditions/)during checkout.

#### **Self-Funded & Forum Access Category**

Please note that participation slots in the **Self-Funded** and **Forum Admission** categories are **nontransferable** and cannot be assigned to another individual.

Delegates who have paid the full registration fee for these categories may be eligible for a refund, in the form of a **GYN** **Credit Voucher (GYNCV)**, provided specific conditions are met.

#### **GYN Credit Voucher (GYNCV) Eligibility & Conditions**

To qualify for a **Credit Voucher (CV)**, delegates must meet the following criteria and adhere to the cancellation timeline:

#### **General Requirements**

* **Payment Method:** The conference fee must be paid in **full** via a single transaction. Installment payments are not eligible for credit vouchers.
* **Submission:** All requests for credit vouchers must be submitted formally via **email**.

#### **Cancellation & Refund Schedule**

The amount of credit issued depends on how far in advance the request is received before the first day of the event:

| **Notice Period** | **Cancellation Fee** | **CVV Issued** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **45+ Days Prior** | 0 | **Full Remaining Balance** |
| **30 Days Prior** | 50% | **50% of the Remaining Balance** |
| **15 Days Prior** | 75% | **25% of the Remaining Balance** |
| **Less than 15 Days** | 100% | **No Voucher Issued** |

#### **Refund Process**

###### To request a refund or credit voucher, please email contact@thegyn.org with the subject line: **“Refund Request – [Conference Name]”**

###### Include the following information:

* **Full Name**
* **Order Number**
* **Reason for Refund Request**
* **Supporting Documentation** (if applicable)

#### **Travel Reimbursement Policy for Fully Funded Delegates**

* **Timing**: Reimbursements of airfare are issued only after the conclusion of the event.
* **Requirements:** Delegates must provide accurate bank information and necessary details
* **Processing Time:** Payments will be processed within 4 weeks of the event conclusion.

#### **Unforeseen Events**

While registration fees are generally non-refundable, GYN acknowledges that emergencies like natural disasters or political unrest may occur. In such instances, we will review refund requests individually to determine if an exception can be made.

**Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.**
