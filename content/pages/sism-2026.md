##### **What is the SDG Innovation Summit 2026?**

**The SDG Innovation Summit Malaysia 2026 is a premier international youth summit designed to bring together 100 young leaders, innovators, students, entrepreneurs, professionals, researchers, and changemakers from around the world to explore innovative ideas, foster meaningful collaboration, and create sustainable impact in support of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).**

**Following the remarkable success of our [international summit in Bangkok](https://thegyn.org/fms-thailand/), Thailand, the journey now continues in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia—a vibrant hub of innovation, education, entrepreneurship, and sustainable development.**

##### **What Will You Experience?**

**Through keynote addresses, high-level panel discussions, leadership workshops, innovation labs, strategic networking, cultural exchange activities, and an exclusive institutional visit to Asia Pacific University (APU), Malaysia, delegates will gain valuable knowledge, practical skills, global perspectives, and meaningful international connections.**

## Program Purpose

The **SDG Innovation Summit Malaysia** **aims** to empower the next generation of global leaders by creating a collaborative platform where innovation meets purpose and ideas become action.

The summit inspires young leaders to present innovative ideas aligned with SDG 4 (Quality Education), SDG 5 (Gender Equality), SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure), and SDG 13 (Climate Action).

## SUMMIT FOCUS AREAS

SDG 4:

Empowering through Quality Education.

SDG 5:

Breaking barriers for Gender Equality.

SDG 9:

Driving Innovation & Resilient Infrastructure.

SDG 13:

Taking urgent action for our Climate.

## $1,000 SEED GRANT AWARD

As part of the SDG Innovation Summit Malaysia 2026, participants will compete for a **$1,000 Seed Grant** to support innovative projects and social impact initiatives. Whether your project is at the conceptual stage, in its pilot phase, currently under implementation, or fully realized, we welcome you to present your innovation on stage.

## CO-HOSTED BY UNU GLOBAL HEALTH

The Global Youth Network (GYN) organizes the SDG Innovation Summit Malaysia, co-hosted by the United Nations University – International Institute for Global Health, Malaysia. ## Have Questions? Check the FAQs.

When will the selected applicants be announced?

The result will be announced on **16th October 2026**.

How Can I Apply for Fully Funded and Partially Funded Slots?

To apply for a fully or partially funded scholarship, please complete the general application form. All submissions are reviewed by our independent, international selection panel, which ensures a transparent process for final scholarship decisions.

Can I Attend the SDG Innovation Summit If Not Selected for the Scholarship?

If you aren’t selected for a scholarship, you can still join us at the summit by choosing either the Self-Funded Package or the Forum Access Package. Regardless of your funding status, you will enjoy the same full experience and privileges as all other participants.

Does GYN Provide Visa Assistance?

To help with your visa application, the GYN will provide an official invitation letter, proof of accommodation, and the program location. While we are happy to offer guidance throughout the process, please note that the final decision rests with the embassy, and we cannot guarantee visa approval.

What should I do if I do not have a passport at the time of application?

You can still apply! Simply upload a copy or enter the number of your National Identity Card (NIC), or any student ID in place of the passport details for now.

How can I pay my application fee?

You can securely pay the application fee using a debit or credit card, Google Pay through a trusted Stripe payment gateway.

Why is the application fee?

Application fees are standard for many international conferences and youth programs. The fee supports our application, review, and selection process and helps us manage applications from participants worldwide. It does not guarantee selection.

Am I required to provide proof of English language proficiency?

No, English test scores (such as IELTS or TOEFL) are not required for this application.

Need Assistance or Want to Contact Us?

You can reach us via email at **contact@thegyn.org**

How will I find out about my selection and results?

All applicants will receive an email with their results. We will also post the final list of selected delegates on the GYN Facebook and Instagram pages. Follow us on ***[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/thegyn.org/)*** and ***[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/gyn_global)***

What is the application deadline?

The application deadline is **11th October 2026.**
