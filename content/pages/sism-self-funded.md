### **Self-Funded Application Form (Confirmed Seat)**

The **SDG Innovation Summit Malaysia 2026** is a premier international youth summit that brings together **100 outstanding participants** from around the world, including young leaders, innovators, students, entrepreneurs, professionals, researchers, and changemakers. 

Taking place in **Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia**, from **19–22 November 2026**, the summit offers participants the opportunity to learn from experts, build global networks, enhance their leadership skills, and collaborate on creating sustainable impact for communities worldwide.

* **Event Dates:** November 19–22, 2026
