# Personas and conversion map

Built from what the site actually sells: scholarship applications ($18.50 fee), paid seats ($199 Forum Access, $399 Self-Funded, $49 invitation letter), and partnerships. Eligibility per the SISM page: worldwide, 17–50, any field, no IELTS/TOEFL.

## 1. Amina, first-time applicant
- 22, final-year student in Lagos or Dhaka or Kathmandu. Found GYN through an Instagram reel or Opportunities Radar. Mostly on a mid-range Android phone on mobile data.
- **Wants:** to know if it's legit, what's covered, whether she qualifies, and how to apply before the deadline.
- **Worries:** "Is this a scam?" The application fee and visa questions are the trust test. Needs to see the UNU Global Health co-host, real past-delegate videos, real faces.
- **Primary action:** apply for a scholarship.
- **Shortest path:** Home hero → "Apply for SISM 2026" → event page anchored at funding options → choose category → 3-step form. 2 taps from landing to form.
- **Design implications:** lightweight on mobile data (3D must be optional), trust signals above the fold (co-host, dates, venue, seats), FAQ answers for fee/visa/passport inline, deadline visible but not shouty.

## 2. Daniel, returning delegate / ambassador
- 27, attended Future Minds Summit Bangkok, now a GYN Global Ambassador. Shares links with his network.
- **Wants:** the next summit, a link to send friends, to see himself in the highlight videos, to be recognised.
- **Primary action:** watch and share, then register for a confirmed seat (Forum Access / Self-Funded) or refer via the affiliate program.
- **Shortest path:** Home → Videos (deep-linkable player, share button) → "Next summit" card → ticket tier → form. Or a deep link straight to a video or ticket tier.
- **Design implications:** every video and every ticket tier has a shareable URL; ambassadors page shows faces and flags; referral (Rewardful) link is findable, not buried in the footer.

## 3. Dr. Farah, partner / sponsor / institution
- Programme lead at a university, NGO, or a company like the ones in the partner strip. Evaluating whether to co-host, send a cohort, or speak.
- **Wants:** credibility at a glance: who's on the advisory board, past events, scale (countries, delegates), the UN co-host, a contact route that reaches a human.
- **Primary action:** contact for partnership or group enrolment ("Group Discounts Available … email contact@thegyn.org").
- **Shortest path:** Home → "Partner with us" in nav → About (board, impact, past summits) → contact form with a partnership topic preselected.
- **Design implications:** a calmer, credible register in About/Partner sections; numbers shown only where the content supplies them (we don't invent stats); downloadable deck later.
