### Verdict per concept

**E Full Spectrum.** Strongest colour energy and the only one with an FAQ and programme, but it breaks on a phone (no menu, clipped H1), ignores reduced motion, and is the heaviest page at 5.7 MB.
It also opens a Malaysia summit with a Bangkok photo and its footer logo vanishes; not showable on mobile as is.

**F Hand in Hand.** Warmest and most human, but the hero puts a "Kuala Lumpur" sticker on a Bangkok photo and collapses into overlapping text on mobile.
The copy invents the most ("proved the model", "best team project", "eight countries"); trust risk is the highest of the four.

**G Deep Field.** Cleanest structure, lightest page and the most credible register for UNU, but its selling point, the globe animation, never runs, the theme toggle is dead, and the mobile hero is clipped.
It also misnames UNU, has no real photo in the hero, fails AA on hero text, and drifts away from the logo's colours. Fixable in a day, but it must be fixed before it goes into the app.

**H Poster Club (in progress during this pass).** Most distinctive and the best ticket layout idea, but the $199 button is invisible, stickers overlap the facts and teleport when dragged, and the APU section uses a Bangkok photo.
No mobile menu and every nav and policy link is `#`; not demo-ready in its 07:41 state.
